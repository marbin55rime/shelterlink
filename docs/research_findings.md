# Research findings

What we learn from field work. Each finding should change how ShelterLink is designed.

## F1. Children go to the nearest government hospital, but usually alone (2026-10-22)
**What we found:** When sick, Alor School students go to the nearest government hospital (Dhaka Shishu, Suhrawardy, DMCH, BMU). Hospitals expect a child to come with a trusted adult, but most street children have no parent or guardian with them.

**Other problems:**
- The OPD ticket counter closes at 2:00 pm, and OPD is closed on Friday.
- Free medicine runs out, and children may have no money to buy it.

**What ShelterLink should do:**
1. Tell children the hospital hours **before** they go (don't send them on a Friday or after 2 pm unless it's an emergency).
2. Suggest going with a **trusted volunteer** (e.g. an Alor School teacher), and give **1098** if no adult is available.
3. Emergencies: **go straight to the Emergency gate**, never wait for an adult.
4. Future idea: a **"health buddy" list** of trained volunteers who can go with a child to the hospital.
5. Future idea: list pharmacies or NGOs that give **free medicine** when the hospital runs out.

## F2. Children are all over Dhaka, but checked services are only around Dhanmondi (2026-10-29)
**What we found:** ShelterLink now knows 88 areas across Dhaka (map locations from OpenStreetMap), and can also use GPS from a phone or kiosk. But all verified services are in the centre of the city (Dhanmondi, Shahbag, Agargaon, Bakshibazar). A child in Uttara is sent to a hospital about 11 km away (over 3 hours walking).

**What ShelterLink does now:** shows distance and walking time, and warns when the nearest checked place is far (over 5 km).

**What is needed:** verified food, health and education services in every part of Dhaka, especially Mirpur, Uttara, Jatrabari, Sadarghat, Kamalapur and Badda. This is a data task: partner NGOs in those areas could each check and add their local services.
