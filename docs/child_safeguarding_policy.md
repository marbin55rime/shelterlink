# Child safeguarding policy (DRAFT v0.1)

> This is a draft. Ask a partner NGO or a child-protection professional to review it before any pilot.

## 1. Purpose
ShelterLink helps street children find free **food, health care and education**. Children's safety is more important than any feature.

## 2. Data we never collect
- Names, photos, faces, or videos of children
- Phone numbers of children
- Exact sleeping places or location history
- Family details

## 3. Data we keep (anonymous only)
- What was asked about (food, health, education) and the general area (landmark)
- Whether the answer was helpful (👍 / 👎)
- Voice recordings are **deleted right after** they are turned into text

## 4. Only verified services
- Every service is checked in person or by phone before it is listed.
- No one can add a service without review by the project lead or a partner NGO.
- Any complaint about a service means it is hidden right away until it is checked again.

## 5. Emergencies always come first
If a child mentions danger, injury, abuse, being lost, or serious illness:
1. The bot immediately gives the **child helpline** number: ______ (fill in for your country, e.g. 1098 in India and Nepal)
2. And the **ambulance / police** numbers: ______
3. The case is flagged for a partner NGO to follow up.
4. When unsure, treat it as an emergency.

## 6. Health rules
- The bot **never** diagnoses illness or recommends medicines.
- It only points to free health services and emergency numbers.

## 7. Human oversight
- A trained adult reviews flagged conversations every day during the pilot.
- Volunteers using ShelterLink with children follow this policy.
